# Imagery

## Current state

Every image on the site is an **original illustrated plate**, generated by code in `scripts/plates/`. They were made because no
licensed photography was available while the site was built. They depict landscapes in general terms (a volcano, a cloud forest, a
cove) and are **not** pictures of specific properties, partners, people or experiences. Their alt text says that they are
illustrations.

The plates share the brand palette, so they look deliberate. Photography is still the long-term goal: the brief puts it at the centre of
the experience.

## Replacing them with photography

All images are referenced by key through `src/content/images.ts`. To swap one:

1. Add the licensed photo to `public/images/` (JPEG or WebP, ideally at least 2400px on the long edge for full-bleed use). `next/image`
   resizes and serves modern formats automatically.
2. Update the entry's `src`, `width`, `height` and `alt`. Describe what is actually in the photo.
3. Optionally set `position` (CSS `object-position`) to control the crop. Images are cropped to many ratios across the site: full-bleed
   heroes, 4:5 portraits, 16:10 thumbnails and squares.

No component changes are needed.

### Photography guidance

- Natural light, real texture, a strong sense of place. Avoid over-saturated stock clichés.
- Mix wide landscapes, portrait crops, architecture and intimate details.
- Don't use photos of specific hotels or lodges unless you have the rights **and** a real relationship with the property. Don't imply
  partnerships an image can't support.
- Keep a record of licence and credit for each image.

## Regenerating the plates

```bash
npm i -D playwright            # or point PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE at an existing install
node scripts/plates/render.mjs # all plates → public/images/plates/*.jpg
node scripts/plates/render.mjs arenal osa   # selected plates
node scripts/plates/render-og.mjs          # public/og.jpg social card
```
